# Historical swarm protocol — retired

The Dynamic Swarm / Foundry / `swarm-control` operating model previously documented here is retired for normal UNRENDERED development.

Current execution authority is the repository-root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Agents should coordinate through live GitHub truth — `main`, branches, pull requests, checks, reviews, issues, and exact runtime/Studio evidence — rather than worker IDs, claim leases, fencing tokens, admission rules, synthetic capacity, or a custom coordination branch.

Historical swarm commits, branches, events, and documents may still be consulted when they contain unique product research or evidence. They do not grant or deny ownership of ordinary repository work.

The product-quality bar has **not** been reduced. `Docs/QUALITY_STANDARD.md`, `Docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `Docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `Docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` continue to define product direction, architecture, dependencies, and Reality-Grade evidence requirements.
