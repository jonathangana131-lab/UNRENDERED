# Procedural Systems

## Principle

Procedural does not mean random. Infinite realism comes from constrained grammars, histories, deterministic recipes, and selective violations.

## Reconstruction pipeline

WorldSeed
-> MacroRealityField
-> RegionIntent
-> FakeHistory
-> TopologyGraph
-> StructuralAssembly
-> MaterialHistory
-> FurnishingHistory
-> ObjectPopulation
-> NarrativeIncidents
-> Atmosphere
-> Anomalies
-> Traces
-> Entities / Still Lifes
-> FirstObservationLock

## VibeVector

A region carries continuous dimensions rather than one biome enum:
- normality,
- familiarity,
- nostalgia,
- comfort,
- loneliness,
- dreamness,
- oppression,
- beauty,
- sterility,
- decay,
- humidity,
- warmth,
- darkness,
- vastness,
- claustrophobia,
- temporal wrongness,
- visual instability,
- acoustic emptiness,
- human trace,
- biological wrongness,
- electrical instability.

Systems consume the same vector so visuals/audio/architecture do not contradict one another accidentally.

## Reality Debt

Major anomalies consume a regional wrongness budget. After a huge impossible event, the generator may deliberately become very normal. This gives horror rhythm instead of constant escalation.

## Familiarity Debt

Too much novelty increases pressure toward repeated/familiar motifs. Too much repetition earns novelty. Repetition is a controlled pacing tool.

## Memory contamination

Previously observed motifs can leak into later regions as abstract `MemoryMotif`s: chair family, clock time, room proportion, color, layout motif. Later echoes can become more complete but slightly wrong.

## Architectural history

Generate apparent build era, remodels, repairs, water damage, replaced carpet, patched walls, abandoned wiring, obsolete signage, and maintenance quality before adding uncanny errors.

## Violation grammar

Examples:
- impossible internal window,
- one extra stair,
- exit sign toward a wall,
- ceiling grid alignment that cannot continue,
- carpet climbing a recess,
- corridor with impossible visible length,
- room larger than exterior constraint.

Keep violations sparse.

## Furniture / ObjectGenome

Furniture is generated from construction graphs, not neural-mesh randomness.

An office chair genome can specify:
- manufacturer family,
- apparent era,
- dimensions,
- five-star/cantilever/stacking base,
- caster family,
- seat/back construction,
- arms,
- materials,
- fasteners,
- mass,
- mechanism graph,
- wear.

The normal object must look manufacturable before an anomaly pass can make it subtly wrong.

## Fictional manufacturers

Generate consistent fake manufacturers/product lines so the world develops material culture. Related objects reuse design language, fasteners, plastics, upholstery, and era traits. Rare violations of an established design language become meaningful.

## Still Lifes

Generate scene intent first:
1. believable setting,
2. plausible activity,
3. plausible furniture/participants,
4. solve contacts/poses,
5. settle physical composition,
6. spend a small violation budget.

A Still Life may be purely static, physically collapsible, observation-reactive, or capable of transitioning to an entity. Players are not told which.

## EntityGenome

Controlled body plans define anatomy, masses, joints, sensors, actuators, locomotion and behavior ranges. Individual temperament varies curiosity, aggression, persistence, fear, territoriality and sensory bias. Do not generate anatomy that cannot be physically validated.

## Seed regressions

Every generator bug is recorded as:
- world seed,
- generation version,
- region address,
- subsystem salt,
- local key.

Canonical test seeds must remain stable unless an explicit version migration changes them.
